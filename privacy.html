PRIVACY

Privacy

I run this site myself. I use what you submit only to reply to you or to send the AI This Week newsletter you asked for — never to build a profile, and never to sell or share with anyone for their own marketing.

Getting in touch and the newsletter

There is no contact form on this site — the contact section links straight to my email address, so a message you send me arrives in my inbox and nowhere else.

The newsletter form collects your email address, which is processed and stored by Netlify, which hosts this site.

The newsletter uses double opt-in: subscribing sends you one confirmation email, and nothing is added to the send list until you click the link in it. If somebody enters your address by mistake, ignoring that email is enough — you will never receive the digest.

Analytics

I count pageviews with GoatCounter, which sets no cookies and does not track you across sites. It records aggregate counts — page, referrer, browser, and country — and I use them only to see which pages people find useful. I also record which links and buttons get clicked, as counts, not tied to any individual.

The AI chat widget

"Ask me anything" sends the question you type — and the last few messages of that conversation, for context — to Groq, which runs the model that writes the reply. I do not store your questions, but they do leave this site to reach Groq, so please do not type anything personal or sensitive into it.

Security reporting

The site asks your browser to report content-security-policy violations back to me. Those reports record which security rule was broken and the page address that triggered it. They contain no form input, cookies, or message content.

How long I keep things

Emails you send me: only as long as I need them to reply. Newsletter addresses: until you unsubscribe or ask me to delete them. Unsubscribing records your address on a suppression list so that a later import cannot accidentally re-add you.

Your choices

Every newsletter carries a one-click unsubscribe link. For access, correction, or deletion of anything else, email pranavdhawan99@gmail.com and I will action it myself.
