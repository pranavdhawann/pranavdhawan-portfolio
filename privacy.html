PRIVACY

Privacy

I run this site myself. It has no forms, no accounts, and no cookies — I never build a profile of visitors, and never sell or share anything with anyone for their own marketing.

Getting in touch

There is no contact form on this site — the contact section links straight to my email address, so a message you send me arrives in my inbox and nowhere else.

Analytics

I count pageviews with GoatCounter, which sets no cookies and does not track you across sites. It records aggregate counts — page, referrer, browser, and country — and I use them only to see which pages people find useful. I also record which links and buttons get clicked, as counts, not tied to any individual.

The AI chat widget

"Ask me anything" sends the question you type — and the last few messages of that conversation, for context — to OpenAI, which runs the model that writes the reply. The text of each question is also written to this site's server logs at Netlify, my hosting provider, so I can see what people ask. Nothing else about you is logged alongside it, but please do not type anything personal or sensitive into it.

Security reporting

The site asks your browser to report content-security-policy violations back to me. Those reports record which security rule was broken and the page address that triggered it. They contain no form input, cookies, or message content.

How long I keep things

Emails you send me: only as long as I need them to reply. The site previously offered an email newsletter, which has been discontinued; no further emails are sent, and you can ask me to delete an address you signed up with at any time.

Your choices

For access, correction, or deletion of anything, email pranavdhawan99@gmail.com and I will action it myself.
